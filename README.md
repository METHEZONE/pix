# Pix — iPad Overlay Character

A native SwiftUI character that lives on your iPadOS like Desktop Goose.

## How to get it running on your iPad

### Option A — Swift Playgrounds (no Mac needed)
1. Install **Swift Playgrounds** (free on App Store)
2. Copy `Pix.swiftpm` to your iPad (AirDrop, Files app, iCloud Drive, etc.)
3. Open it in Swift Playgrounds → tap **Run**
4. Use **Slide Over** to overlay Pix on top of any other app

### Option B — Xcode (Mac)
1. Open `Pix.swiftpm` in Xcode
2. Select your iPad as target device
3. Run → Pix installs as a real app

## Overlay / Slide Over setup
1. Open Pix
2. Swipe up from the bottom to go home (don't close Pix)
3. Open another app (Safari, Notes, etc.)
4. Swipe from the right edge → Pix appears as a floating panel on top

## What Pix does
- **Auto-walks** back and forth on her own
- **Tap Pix** directly → she jumps and reacts
- **Tap anywhere** → she walks to that spot
- **Changes mood**: happy 💜, scared 😱, sleepy 💤
- **Speech bubbles** with random personality
- **Idle behaviour**: randomly walks, talks, naps, startles
